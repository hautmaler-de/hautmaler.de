# Nice-to-have backlog

The following ideas are deliberately not implemented. There are no placeholders,
dependencies, empty APIs, or backend stubs for them in this repository.

- Appointment booking
- Deposits and payments
- Digital consent forms
- Aftercare portal
- Content management system
- Individual artist profiles
- Waiting list
- Additional languages
- Review integration
- Instagram synchronization
- Gift vouchers
- Availability calendar
- Customer account

Each item would require its own product, privacy, security, operational, and legal
decision before implementation.
